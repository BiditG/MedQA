## The 60-Day Shape

A 60-day plan is for students who already know some basics. It needs discipline, selective coverage, and honest mock analysis.

> With 60 days, every study block must either build marks or repair mistakes.

## Days 1 to 30

- Finish high-yield weak chapters first.
- Rotate Physics, Chemistry, Biology, and MAT every two days.
- Solve MCQs after each topic, not at the end of the week.

## Days 31 to 50

- Increase mixed tests.
- Review the error log before choosing what to study.
- Give extra revision to Biology, Inorganic Chemistry, formulas, and diagrams.

## Days 51 to 60

- Use full mocks and short revision sheets.
- Stop chasing new material.
- Focus on accuracy, sleep, and calm exam rhythm.
