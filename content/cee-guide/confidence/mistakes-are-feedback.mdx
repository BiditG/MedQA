## Mistakes Are Data

Wrong answers are not proof that you cannot do the exam. They are information about what to repair next.

> A mistake becomes useful only after it is named.

## Mistake Categories

- Concept gap.
- Memory gap.
- Formula error.
- Calculation error.
- Misreading.
- Time pressure.

## Repair Method

- Write the correct idea in one sentence.
- Redo the question after 24 to 48 hours.
- Add similar questions for practice.
- Track repeated mistakes separately.

## Mindset

- Do not hide from wrong answers.
- Do not attach identity to one mock.
- Use mistakes to choose tomorrow's study.
