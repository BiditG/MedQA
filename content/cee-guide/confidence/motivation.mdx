## Motivation Changes

Motivation rises and falls during preparation. A reliable routine matters more than waiting to feel inspired.

> Discipline is motivation made small enough to repeat.

## Build Momentum

- Start with a 25-minute block.
- Choose one clear task.
- End by writing the next task.
- Track completed work, not only pending work.

## When You Feel Low

- Revise an easy topic to restart confidence.
- Solve a short MCQ set.
- Clean the desk and begin with one page.
- Avoid comparing your timeline with someone else's.

## Long-Term Fuel

- Keep weekly goals visible.
- Celebrate corrected mistakes.
- Notice accuracy improvement, not only rank or score.
