## Understanding Exam Anxiety

Anxiety does not always mean you are unprepared. Often it means the body is preparing for performance. The goal is not to remove all anxiety; the goal is to stay functional.

> Calm is trained through routine.

## What Helps

- Practice mocks at the same time as the real exam.
- Keep a fixed pre-study and pre-mock routine.
- Use slow breathing before starting a paper.
- Ground yourself by naming what you can control: reading, timing, skipping, checking.

## What Hurts

- Last-minute comparison.
- Random new resources.
- Sleep loss.
- Replaying old mistakes without a correction plan.

## Exam-Day Reset

- Pause for one breath.
- Read the question again.
- Solve the next easiest item.
- Let momentum return through action.
