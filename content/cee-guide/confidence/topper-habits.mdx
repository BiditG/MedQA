## What Strong Students Do

High scorers are not perfect. They repeat the basics more honestly and analyze mistakes more carefully.

> Topper habits are usually boring, consistent, and powerful.

## Core Habits

- They revise daily.
- They limit resources.
- They analyze mocks before taking the next one.
- They sleep enough to protect accuracy.
- They keep formulas, diagrams, reactions, and examples visible.

## Test Habits

- They know which questions to skip.
- They read options carefully.
- They do not panic after one hard section.
- They review repeated errors weekly.

## Study Identity

- Be the student who returns to the plan.
- Be the student who fixes one mistake at a time.
- Be the student who keeps showing up.
