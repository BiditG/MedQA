## The Day Before CEE

The day before the exam is for calm consolidation. Heavy new study usually creates more noise than marks.

> Make the day predictable.

## What to Do

- Pack admit card, ID, stationery, and required documents.
- Confirm route, reporting time, and travel buffer.
- Revise only final sheets.
- Eat familiar food.
- Sleep early.

## What to Avoid

- New chapters.
- Full heavy mocks.
- Late-night scrolling.
- Comparing preparation with friends.
- Changing your entire strategy.

## Final Review

- Formulas.
- Biology diagrams.
- Reaction maps.
- Inorganic exceptions.
- MAT pattern notes.
- Error-log highlights.
