## MAT Strategy

MAT is trained through pattern exposure. It includes verbal reasoning, numerical reasoning, logical sequencing, and spatial or abstract reasoning.

> MAT is a skill section. Small daily practice works better than rare long sessions.

## Study Method

- Practice 15 to 20 questions daily.
- Time each set.
- Write the rule behind missed questions.
- Repeat weak pattern types after two days.

## Common Areas

- Analogy, classification, coding-decoding, blood relation, direction sense.
- Number series, missing numbers, ratios, averages, percentages.
- Ranking, arrangements, sequence of events, syllogism-type reasoning.
- Figure series, mirror image, water image, folding, rotation, embedded figures.

## Exam Habit

- Do not stare too long.
- Eliminate options that break the rule.
- Return later if the pattern is not visible quickly.
