## What Physics Demands

Physics rewards clear concepts, correct formulas, and calm calculation. The major areas are mechanics, heat and thermodynamics, waves, optics, electricity, magnetism, electrostatics, capacitors, and modern physics.

> The fastest Physics improvement usually comes from fixing repeated mistakes, not from collecting more formulas.

## Study Method

- Learn the concept through one clean example before attempting MCQs.
- Write formulas with **units, conditions, and common substitutions**.
- Solve numericals in timed sets so calculation speed becomes familiar.
- Review every wrong answer by naming the error: concept, formula, unit, sign, or arithmetic.

## Focus Areas

- Mechanics: vectors, kinematics, laws of motion, work-energy, circular motion, fluids, gravitation, elasticity.
- Heat: thermal expansion, calorimetry, gas laws, first law, second law.
- Electricity: Ohm's law, circuits, capacitors, electrostatics, magnetic field, induction, AC.
- Optics and waves: refraction, lenses, interference, diffraction, stationary waves.
- Modern physics: photoelectric effect, radioactivity, semiconductors, logic gates.

## Exam Habit

- Estimate the answer before doing full calculation.
- Check units before selecting an option.
- Keep a one-page formula sheet for each major unit.
