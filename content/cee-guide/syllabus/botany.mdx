## What Botany Demands

Botany is rich in detail: cell biology, plant diversity, anatomy, physiology, genetics, ecology, reproduction, and applied botany. It becomes scoring when facts are organized visually.

> Botany is not only memory. It is memory arranged through diagrams, tables, and examples.

## Study Method

- Read a topic once for understanding, then close the book and recall the key terms.
- Use diagrams for plant anatomy, cell organelles, reproduction, and physiology.
- Make example lists for plant groups, medicinal plants, adaptations, and economic importance.
- Revise genetics with small problems, not only theory.

## High-Yield Areas

- Cell cycle, mitosis, meiosis, organelles, and chromosomes.
- Plant physiology: photosynthesis, respiration, transpiration, absorption, ascent of sap.
- Plant diversity: fungi, algae, bryophytes, pteridophytes, gymnosperms, angiosperms.
- Genetics, biotechnology, tissue culture, plant breeding, and food security.

## Exam Habit

- Draw one rough diagram daily.
- Convert every example list into flashcards.
- Revise ecology and physiology every week.
