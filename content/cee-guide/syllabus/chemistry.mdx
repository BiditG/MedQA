## What Chemistry Demands

Chemistry is three subjects wearing one coat. Physical Chemistry behaves like Physics, Organic Chemistry follows reaction logic, and Inorganic Chemistry depends on repeated recall.

> Do not study all Chemistry with the same method. Match the method to the branch.

## Physical Chemistry

- Practice mole concept, equilibrium, kinetics, electrochemistry, thermodynamics, solutions, and redox with calculations.
- Write formulas with units and assumptions.
- Redo slow numericals until the setup becomes automatic.

## Organic Chemistry

- Group reactions by functional group transformation.
- Learn reagents by what they do: oxidize, reduce, substitute, eliminate, protect, or activate.
- Build reaction maps instead of isolated lists.

## Inorganic and Analytical Chemistry

- Use comparison tables for periodic trends, compounds, colors, ores, and exceptions.
- Revise qualitative tests and functional group tests in short daily bursts.
- Keep exact wording sharp because factual MCQs often turn on one word.
