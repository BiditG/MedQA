## What Zoology Demands

Zoology covers animal diversity, evolution, selected animals, histology, human physiology, disease, immunity, biotechnology, and conservation. The most scoring chapters are often those you can revise repeatedly with flowcharts.

> In Zoology, sequence matters: pathway, organ, hormone, disease, cause, effect.

## Study Method

- Study human physiology system by system.
- Make flowcharts for digestion, respiration, circulation, excretion, nerves, hormones, and reproduction.
- Use tables for animal classification and phylum features.
- Practice genetics and disease questions after theory.

## High-Yield Areas

- Human physiology and endocrinology.
- Animal diversity from protozoa to chordata.
- Evolution, evidences, and human evolution.
- Immunity, vaccines, infectious diseases, and applied biology.

## Exam Habit

- Recall diagrams before rereading.
- Keep disease tables with organism, transmission, symptoms, and prevention.
- Review animal examples until classification becomes quick.
