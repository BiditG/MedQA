## Smart Guessing

Guessing is useful only when it is controlled. Blind guessing often damages accuracy, but educated guessing after elimination can protect marks.

> Guess when you have evidence. Skip when you only have emotion.

## When to Guess

- You can eliminate at least one or two options.
- The question is from a familiar topic.
- The remaining options can be compared logically.
- You have enough time to review the wording.

## When to Skip

- You have no clue about the concept.
- All options look equally unfamiliar.
- You are guessing because of panic.
- The question is consuming too much time.

## Exam Habit

- Mark doubtful questions.
- Finish sure questions first.
- Return to guessed questions only after easy marks are safe.
