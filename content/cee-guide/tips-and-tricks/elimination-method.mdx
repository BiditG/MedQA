## Elimination Method

Elimination turns a hard question into a smaller decision. It works especially well when options contain extreme words, wrong units, mismatched facts, or impossible values.

> Cross out what cannot be true before searching for what is true.

## What to Eliminate

- Options with incorrect units.
- Options that contradict the question.
- Extreme statements such as always or never when exceptions are known.
- Biology statements with one wrong term.
- Numericals with impossible magnitude.

## How to Practice

- Solve MCQs by explaining why each wrong option is wrong.
- Use elimination during mocks, not only during revision.
- Keep a list of repeated option traps.

## Exam Habit

- Read all options before selecting.
- Recheck negative words like not, except, incorrect, and false.
