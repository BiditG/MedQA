## Revision That Works

Revision should make recall faster. Passive rereading feels productive, but active recall shows what the brain can actually retrieve.

> Recall first. Read after.

## Active Revision Tools

- Blank-page recall.
- Flashcards.
- Formula rewriting.
- Diagram labeling.
- Reaction maps.
- Error-log review.

## Spaced Revision

- Revise a topic the same day.
- Revisit it after two to three days.
- Revisit it again after one week.
- Increase frequency for topics you keep missing.

## Final Notes

- Keep final notes short.
- Include only formulas, exceptions, diagrams, reactions, examples, and repeated mistakes.
- Do not rewrite the textbook.
