## Time Management

Time management is not only speed. It is choosing the right question at the right moment.

> The first pass should collect marks, not prove bravery.

## Three-Pass Method

- Pass 1: solve sure and quick questions.
- Pass 2: solve moderate questions that need calculation or recall.
- Pass 3: review doubtful and time-consuming questions.

## Section Strategy

- Start with the subject that settles your confidence.
- Keep checkpoints so one subject does not eat the paper.
- Do not spend too long on a single numerical early.
- Save a few minutes for bubbling or final checking.

## Practice Rule

- Use the same time strategy in mocks.
- After each mock, record where time was lost.
- Fix the pattern before the next mock.
