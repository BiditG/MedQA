## Protecting Marks

Negative marking hurts most when students attempt questions from panic, ego, or boredom. A skipped question can be a mature scoring decision.

> Accuracy is a strategy, not a personality trait.

## Confidence Levels

- Sure: answer immediately.
- Probable: attempt after elimination.
- Risky: mark and return later.
- Blind: skip unless the marking scheme makes it worth trying.

## Mistake Triggers

- Rushing easy questions.
- Ignoring negative words.
- Over-attempting after one hard section.
- Changing correct answers without a reason.

## Exam Habit

- Use the first pass for high-confidence questions.
- Attempt probable questions only after eliminating options.
- Leave blind guesses unless your strategy clearly supports them.
